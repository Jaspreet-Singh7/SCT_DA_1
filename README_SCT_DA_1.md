## 📝 Task Description
Use **Microsoft Excel** or Google Sheets to clean and analyze a provided dataset (Superstore Sales Data). Create a dashboard using **Pivot Tables and Charts**.

**Objective:** Visualize Total Sales, Profit by Category, and Sales Trends over time. Use **Conditional Formatting** to highlight high/low performers.

---

## ✅ What I Did

### 📂 Sheet 1 — Raw Data
- Loaded **494 rows** of Superstore Sales data
- 19 columns including Order ID, Sales, Profit, Category, Region, Segment
- Applied **Auto Filter** and **Alternating Row Colors**
- Froze header row for easy navigation

### 📊 Sheet 2 — Pivot Summary
- **Sales & Profit by Category** using SUMIF formulas
- **Sales by Region** with % of total
- **Monthly Sales Trend** (2022–2024) using SUMPRODUCT
- **Sales by Segment** analysis

### 🎯 Sheet 3 — Dashboard
- **5 KPI Cards** — Total Sales, Profit, Orders, Avg Discount, Profit Margin
- **4 Interactive Charts:**
  - Bar Chart — Sales & Profit by Category
  - Pie Chart — Sales Distribution by Region
  - Line Chart — Monthly Sales Trend 2022–2024
  - Bar Chart — Sales & Profit by Segment

### 🎨 Sheet 4 — Conditional Formatting
- **Color Scale** on Sales and Profit columns
- **⭐ High Performer** — Green highlight (Profit Margin > 20%)
- **⚠️ Low Performer** — Red highlight (Profit Margin < 5%)
